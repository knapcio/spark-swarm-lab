# DEVELOPMENT recipe selection

Winner-biased development selection on 8 reused tasks x 3 seeds; not fresh generalization or fleet throughput.

Selected: **scribe_full**. Strict development success improvement.

The frozen rule requires strictly more successes than index_original. Cost tiers apply globally only when all168 cases have the required complete measurement coverage; skipped-tier totals remain null, never zero. Final ties use listed variant order. No selection occurs on an incomplete matrix.

Eight reused development tasks, three repetitions each;5000 task-cluster replicates retain all repetitions. Paired contrasts are pointwise/unadjusted, not new independent evidence after choosing the variant on these same outcomes. All-pass/all-fail empirical intervals may degenerate and do not imply a true population guarantee.

Conditional conversion is verified within the case budget divided by all observed eligible cases, including denied/unfinished/deadline commits. Unknown first responses are counted separately; do not treat an unobserved response as zero eligibility. Empty-notes still receives the full task and is a control, not a chance-only impossibility. Finish rate or zero-denial counts do not establish quality.

Measured input/output, client lower bounds, server coverage, case wall/deadlines and lifecycle counts remain separate. No GPU-equivalent cost or mixed-queue fleet throughput is inferred. All raw benchmark inputs/source remain unchanged.
