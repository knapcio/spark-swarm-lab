This is a posthoc diagnostic of already-seen Nemotron arithmetic evidence. It preserves the original scores and does not constitute a new experiment, an unseen test, or an implemented routing policy. No Fable outputs were read, no model requests were made, and no puzzle search or generated code was executed. `diagnostic.py` reads retained JSON traces and exact-verifier outcome events.

The most immediate limitation is answer emission within the frozen budgets. Direct ON8k scored 18/72;52 of its 54 failures ended at the token limit with nonempty reasoning and null answer content, and 2 were rejected nonpositive intermediate results. E4 scored 49/72;22 of its 23 unsuccessful cases had four such null-content length finishes. Its remaining unsuccessful case had three length finishes and one legal plan that missed the target. Among all retained E4 responses, 49 verified successes, 94 null-content length/parse failures and 1 legal miss were observed.

Strong sequential solo scored 45/72. It allowed up to four 8192-token plans, with 32768 completion tokens maximum and a 180-second whole-case deadline, but never dispatched a fourth plan. It dispatched 150 requests and retained 123 responses. Plan 1: 72 responses, 25 successes / 47 failures. Plan 2: 47 responses, 16 successes / 31 failures. Plan 3: 31 dispatches, 4 retained successful responses and 27 responses absent at the deadline. Every one of the 27 unsuccessful cases retained two null-content 8192-token length failures, then reached the 180-second deadline during its third request. Fourth requests were not dispatched. The 45 successful cases stopped after plans 1/2/3 in 25/16/4 cases. Across all retained solo responses, 74 length finishes had reasoning but null answer content; one additional stop-finished response failed JSON parsing with extra data; three parsed plans had invalid intermediate results.

E4 dispatched all 288 requests, but retained only 144 responses. The 144 absent responses belong to cases stopped after another plan produced a verified success; none belongs to an E4 deadline. This is trace censoring, not proof that the backend canceled each request or performed zero work. Backend/server costs remain whatever the original per-case drain measurements recorded. The retained E4 agent-position counts are 15/12/11/11 successes, 25/23/23/24 verified failures, and 32/37/38/37 unknown outcomes, each out of 72 dispatches. These are not unbiased independent per-agent success rates. Strong solo's third-plan 4/4 retained success rate is likewise survivor-biased because 27 third outcomes are missing.

E4 and strong solo overlap on 39 successes; E4 alone contributes 10 and solo alone 6 relative to that pair. Direct adds 3 successes beyond their union. Relative to E4's 23 failed task-seed pairs, 9 have a recorded success in direct or strong solo, and 14 have a success somewhere among all nine methods. These are lower bounds on observed alternative-plan availability, not predictions for a new router or equal-cost comparison. The method-complementarity CSV includes all 36 method pairs.

Across seeds, E4 succeeded at least once on 21/24 tasks, strong solo 20/24, and direct 14/24. Their union reaches 23/24. The one task missed by all three on every seed is `eva-8955514173a5` (six numbers, target 295); E2 solved its seed 1 and cascade solved its seed 2. E4 alone also missed `eva-c25dd13868a8` and `eva-eea066d2b2d7` across all seeds. Strong solo alone also missed `eva-541797dfa346`, `eva-eea066d2b2d7`, and `eva-eca4c7a9c432`. Across all nine methods, every one of the 24 tasks has at least one recorded success, while 9 specific task-seed pairs remain unsolved by all nine; those pairs are listed in DIAGNOSTIC.json. Reusing an already-known solution across seed labels would leak evaluation answers and cannot establish fresh 72/72 performance.

The evidence supports testing answer-emission/budget strategies as new, explicitly exploratory conditions. A longer whole-case allowance at the same nominal 32768-token ceiling, or adaptive per-call caps, changes the budget/dispatch policy; neither is a demonstrated improvement or a repair for an implementation bug. Existing traces cannot recover the missing E4/solo response bodies, show what a fourth sequential plan would do, or establish how more generation time changes correctness. Per-case CSV preserves actual calls, completed/unfinished requests, deadlines, stop reasons, costs, and timing so those gaps remain visible.
